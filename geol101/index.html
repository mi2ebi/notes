01.2 origin of the solar system

big giant nebula that starts spinning around a bunch ~4.6 bya

nebulae don't just spontaneously start collapsing, they have enough internal pressure to avoid that. so something like a nearby supernova must have caused it. but i guess this isn't an astronomy class

01.3 early history of the earth

earth forms by accretion of some of the debris in our part of the nebula. asteroid impacts create a lot of heat, but not a ton

then around 4.5 bya, a planet the size of mars hits us! it melts the entire planet and some of the impact creates the moon. because earth is a completely molten ball now, the denser molecules sink to the center (planetary differentiation; this also happened to the other terrestrial planets).

01.4 plate tectonics (basics)

analogy with skulls

geologic activity occurs along plate boundaries. there are 7 major plates (area > 20 million km²), 15 minor plates (area > 1 million km²), and "lots" of micro plates (area < 1 million km²). the specific number of plates is a point of contention

the movement of plates is driven by heat transfer / convection inside earth. hot parts of the mantle risw up and cold parts sink. very slowly, it takes millions of years. the convection moves the plates around

types of plate boundaries:

divergent boundary
new lithosphere is being created by rising mantle. plates move away from each other
convergent boundary
lithosphere gets destroyed or folded
transform boundary
plates slide against each other

01.5 physical properties

measurable characteristics of real objects

streak

texture
