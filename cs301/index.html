generative ai is breathing new life into the dead internet theory

dead internet theory (conspiratorial definition)
"the entire world is being gaslit bc all the internet users are really ai!"

yoshija walter says at least these claims are reasonable:

  • algorithms generate much of the internet's content
  • content influences perceptions/behaviors toward algorithmically-driven objectives
  • many internet consumers can't always tell between 'real' and 'fake' data (or human- vs ai-generated)
  • some genai byproducts (eg deepfakses, ai chat) create highly realistic but fabricated content that undermines trust and propels misinformation

reliability is not a primary feature of the internet! look at all the propaganda platforms masquerading as news sites
