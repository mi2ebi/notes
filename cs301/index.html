generative ai is breathing new life into the dead internet theory

dead internet theory (conspiratorial definition)
"the entire world is being gaslit bc all the internet users are really ai!"

yoshija walter says at least these claims are reasonable:

  • algorithms generate much of the internet's content
  • content influences perceptions/behaviors toward algorithmically-driven objectives
  • many internet consumers can't always tell between 'real' and 'fake' data (or human- vs ai-generated)
  • some genai byproducts (eg deepfakses, ai chat) create highly realistic but fabricated content that undermines trust and propels misinformation

reliability is not a primary feature of the internet! look at all the propaganda platforms masquerading as news sites

generative ai in higher education: the good, the bad, and the ugly

good:

  • learning: eg ai is very good at socratic questioning
  • teaching: eg surrogate lecturer, grading
  • reading: eg generating questions
  • writing: eg suggesting rewrites for clarity

bad:

  • fabricated lit reviews
  • misrepresentation of genai output as your own work
  • plagiarism
  • refinement of fake content

ugly: (negative externalities)

  • composing essays/programs: reduced level of understanding what you're doing.
  • labor arbitraging
  • inflated self-efficacy (overconfidence)
  • cognitive decline

some kinds of reviews: exploratory, integrative, critical

one study found that chatgpt4 fabricated 18% of citations (55% for chatgpt3.5). this makes sense, it's a text transformer, rather than information retrieval

gao et al. found that domain specialized reviewrs misclassified ~1/3 of genai-generated abstracts as original
